CeleMod Steam worker uses dynamically linked, unmodified NuGet assemblies:

SteamKit2 3.4.0 — SteamRE contributors — LGPL-2.1-only
Source: https://github.com/SteamRE/SteamKit/tree/1c7bc9c41a529e8fbb1e6890f1e4dbcdc5200cb7
License and bundled third-party notices: licenses/SteamKit2-*.txt in CeleMod's source.
SteamKit2.dll is a separate assembly. No proprietary Steam client or game files are bundled.
Users may replace/rebuild the library and rebuild the APK using scripts/build-android-steam.ps1.

protobuf-net / protobuf-net.Core 3.2.56 — Marc Gravell — Apache-2.0
https://github.com/protobuf-net/protobuf-net/tree/dfdfce61a739cfd76f05fcdacf8a4b3b9e94e684

ZstdSharp.Port 0.8.7 — Oleg Stepanischev and contributors — MIT
https://github.com/oleg-st/ZstdSharp/tree/0ee6121aaa173b42e68d3c6c8816a68e910e0557

System.IO.Hashing 10.0.1 — .NET Foundation and contributors — MIT
https://github.com/dotnet/runtime

Exact transitive dependencies and integrity hashes are pinned in packages.lock.json.

no DepotDownloader source or binaries are included.
